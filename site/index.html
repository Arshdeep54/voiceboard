All the details
Does it work with my favorite app?

Voiceboard copies text to your Linux clipboard and presses Ctrl+Shift+V in the focused application. It works with terminals and apps that accept that shortcut. For other apps, run voiceboard --clipboard and paste manually. Automatic input is designed for X11; native Wayland applications may need different input tooling.

Do I need an app on my phone?

No. Open the pairing URL in Chrome on Android. You can use browser speech recognition, your keyboard’s dictation, or simply type. For a full installable PWA, use Tailscale Serve HTTPS; the README includes the setup.

Is my voice or text stored anywhere?

The Voiceboard receiver receives text, not audio, and doesn’t log or store your prompts. Recognition happens through your phone browser or keyboard; that provider may process speech using its own services. Tailscale carries the text between your paired devices.

Why does it need Tailscale?

Tailscale connects your phone and Linux machine on a private network. Voiceboard listens only on the local Tailscale address by default, with a fresh pairing token each time you start it. Keep the receiver on your trusted tailnet; it’s not designed to be exposed to the public internet.

Is Voiceboard really free?

Yes. Voiceboard is open source under the MIT license, with no subscription or Voiceboard account. You run the receiver on your own machine. Tailscale is a separate service with its own plans.
